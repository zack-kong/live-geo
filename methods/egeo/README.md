# E-GEO

## Evaluated method

The released competitive rewriting prompt from optimized_prompts.json.

- Paper: [E-GEO: A Testbed for Generative Engine Optimization in E-Commerce](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20867)
- Official code: [https://github.com/psbagga17/E-GEO](https://github.com/psbagga17/E-GEO)

## LIVE-GEO adaptation

We applied the author competitive prompt with GPT-4.1 to our product records
and extended its rewriting instructions to the seven reviews. The recorded adapter
used the author optimization entry point and increased its output-token cap from
500 to 1024.
