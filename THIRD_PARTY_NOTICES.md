# Attribution and distribution scope

The five strategy directories link to their original papers and official code.
Implementation and model licenses are available in the corresponding author
repositories.

The BM25 implementation and saved-response parsing functions originate in the
LIVE-GEO experiment scripts. BM25 background:
[Robertson and Zaragoza, The Probabilistic Relevance Framework: BM25 and Beyond](https://doi.org/10.1561/1500000019).
Qwen model information: [Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct).

Apache-2.0 applies to LIVE-GEO-authored package code and documentation.
Model-generated text, responses and third-party material retain their applicable
rights and provider terms.
